The 2026 Green Economy: Inside the $24 Billion Global Coconut Industry Boom

Vietnam Agriculture · Connecting Markets · 63 followers · May 28, 2026

The Coconut Market has been witnessing significant growth as the world embraces the versatility of this tropical fruit. From food and beverages to cosmetics and biofuel, the demand for coconut products is surging across multiple industries.

$19.5–24B Projected global coconut market size 2026
$130B+ Global organic market (growing 10–12% annually)
>70% Asia-Pacific share of global production & consumption

Growth in Emerging Markets

Regions like Asia-Pacific and Latin America are leading the production of coconuts, with countries like India, Indonesia, the Philippines, and Vietnam being top producers. These regions are witnessing rapid growth due to the increasing demand for coconut products globally. According to FiBL, the global organic market has surpassed $130 billion (growing 10–12% annually in 2026), driving clean-label coconut derivatives to surge across the US, EU, and Japan.

Rising Applications in Food & Beverage

In the food industry, coconut is used as an alternative to dairy and processed oils. Coconut water is a favorite among health-conscious consumers, while coconut flour is gaining traction in gluten-free diets. The rising popularity of vegan diets, functional beverages, organic foods, clean-label ingredients, and plant-based dairy alternatives has significantly expanded the commercial scope of coconut-based products.

Sustainable Biofuel from Coconuts

Coconuts are also finding their way into the renewable energy sector, with coconut biodiesel emerging as a sustainable alternative to fossil fuels. AIPSI highlights high expectations for CME (Coconut Methyl Ester) — a coconut-based biofuel enabling corporations to realize Net-Zero targets and ESG commitments.

Organic and Fair-Trade Coconut Products

The shift towards organic and fair-trade practices in coconut farming is becoming a key trend. Consumers are increasingly demanding ethically sourced products, leading companies to focus on sustainable farming and transparent supply chains.

"The modern consumer is actively seeking products that are natural and minimally processed, dairy-free and vegan-friendly, rich in nutrients and electrolytes, sustainable and plant-based. Coconut products align perfectly with these preferences."

Asia-Pacific: The Global Coconut Powerhouse

The Asia-Pacific region dominates the global coconut market, accounting for more than 70% of worldwide production and consumption.

Indonesia

Remains one of the world's largest coconut producers, heavily focused on bulk coconut oil production, export-oriented processing, and industrial coconut derivatives.

Philippines

Has established itself as a leader in virgin coconut oil, desiccated coconut, coconut milk products, and organic coconut exports.

India

India's coconut industry is expanding beyond traditional consumption into packaged coconut water, plant-based food products, premium coconut oils, and coconut-based snacks.

Thailand and Vietnam (Mekong Delta)

Both countries are witnessing strong growth in coconut water exports, especially to North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Korea. Their premium positioning in organic and natural beverages is helping regional brands gain global visibility.

Coconut Water: A Mainstream Functional Beverage

Consumers are increasingly replacing carbonated drinks with healthier hydration alternatives. Coconut water is naturally rich in electrolytes, potassium, magnesium, and antioxidants. Major global beverage companies — Vita Coco, PepsiCo (ONE Coconut Water), Coca-Cola (Zico) — continue expanding their distribution channels to capture rising global demand.

A Strong Decade Ahead

By 2032, the market is expected to exceed USD 24 billion, driven by expanding coconut water consumption, growth in vegan food categories, increased demand for natural ingredients, and rising health awareness worldwide. Asia-Pacific will continue leading production and innovation, while North America and Europe will remain key high-value consumption markets.
