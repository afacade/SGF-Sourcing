Growing Media

Giá Thể Trồng · 2 products
Coco Peat Mụn Dừa PP-wrapped blocks, shrink-wrapped on pallets · MOQ 1 × 40ft HC container +

Coco Peat is milled from the fibrous husk of mature Mekong Delta coconuts, then screened and washed to draw out natural salts before compression. Its high water-holding capacity, stable open structure and near-neutral pH make it a clean, renewable substitute for sphagnum peat — used in hydroponics, seedling nurseries, potting mixes, mushroom substrate and soil conditioning. Supplied washed, low-EC washed, or buffered to your growing programme.

Coco Chips Dừa Cắt Khúc (Coco Chips) 5 kg compressed block · MOQ 1 × 40ft HC container +

Coco Chips are cut from the same coconut husk as our coco peat but left coarse, delivering the air porosity that fine media cannot. Washed to reduce salt content and screened to a uniform grade, they drain freely while still holding moisture at the root zone — the standard medium for orchids, anthurium, berries and long-cycle hydroponic crops, and equally at home as landscape mulch or animal bedding.

Frozen Coconut Milk is pressed from fresh mature coconut kernel and frozen within hours — never heat-sterilised, never canned. Because the cold chain does the preserving, the milk needs no emulsifier, stabiliser or preservative to hold together, and it carries none of the cooked or metallic notes that UHT and canned product pick up on the shelf. What thaws is what was pressed: sweet, full-bodied, and naturally separating into cream and milk. Supplied at standard, light or first-press fat levels for curry pastes, desserts, ice cream, beverages and ready-meal manufacture.
